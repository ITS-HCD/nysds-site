## “Giant”

The winning vote during Office Hours was for naming this release “Giant” after the 12<sup>th</sup> highest peak in New York: Giant Mountain. The Mountain has an elevation of 4,627 ft (1,410 m). That is 15 Statues of Liberty 🗽 (plus a Yankee Stadium foul pole ⚾) tall! 

[Learn more about Giant Mountain <nys-icon name="open_in_new"></nys-icon>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Wolfjaw_Mountain)
